Perimeter Formulas

The perimeter is the total length of a figure's boundary. Here are the formulas for rectangles, squares, triangles, parallelograms, rhombuses, trapezoids, regular polygons and the circumference of a circle.

    Perimeter of a rectangle

    Grade 5
    P=2⋅(a+b)P = 2 \cdot (a + b)P=2⋅(a+b)

    Sum of the lengths of all sides of a rectangle.

    Perimeter of an arbitrary polygon

    Grade 5
    P=a1+a2+a3+a4+a5P = a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + a_4 + a_5P=a1​+a2​+a3​+a4​+a5​

    The perimeter of any polygon equals the sum of the lengths of all its sides. Shown for a pentagon — for any other number of sides the sides are added the same way.
