    Finding 1% of a number

    Grade 5
    A100\frac{A}{100}100A​

    One hundredth of a given number A.

    Legend:
    • A — Number A

    Finding p% of a number A

    Grade 5
    B=A⋅p100B = A \cdot \frac{p}{100}B=A⋅100p​

    The value of a given percentage p of a number A.

    Legend:
    • A — Number A
    • p — Percentage p

    Finding a number from its percentage

    Grade 5
    A=B⋅100pA = B \cdot \frac{100}{p}A=B⋅p100​

    The number A, given that B is p% of it.

    Legend:
    • B — Known part (B)
    • p — Percentage p

    Multiplying powers

    Grade 7
    am⋅an=am+na^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}am⋅an=am+n

    When multiplying powers with the same base, the exponents are added.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a
    • m — Exponent m
    • n — Exponent n

    Dividing powers

    Grade 7
    am:an=am−na^m : a^n = a^{m-n}am:an=am−n

    When dividing powers with the same base, the exponents are subtracted.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a
    • m — Exponent m
    • n — Exponent n

    Power of a power

    Grade 7
    (am)n=am⋅n(a^m)^n = a^{m \cdot n}(am)n=am⋅n

    When raising a power to a power, the exponents are multiplied.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a
    • m — Exponent m
    • n — Exponent n

    Power of a product

    Grade 7
    (a⋅b)n=an⋅bn(a \cdot b)^n = a^n \cdot b^n(a⋅b)n=an⋅bn

    Each factor is raised to the given power.

    Legend:
    • a — Factor a
    • b — Factor b
    • n — Exponent n

    Zero exponent

    Grade 7
    a0=1(a≠0)a^0 = 1 \quad (a \neq 0)a0=1(a=0)

    Any nonzero number raised to the power of 0 equals 1.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a (a≠0)

    Properties of a square root (multiplication)

    Grade 8
    a⋅b=a⋅b(a,b≥0)\sqrt{a \cdot b} = \sqrt{a} \cdot \sqrt{b} \quad (a, b \ge 0)a⋅b​=a​⋅b​(a,b≥0)

    The root of a product of non-negative numbers equals the product of their roots.

    Legend:
    • a — Number a (a≥0)
    • b — Number b (b≥0)

    Properties of a square root (division)

    Grade 8
    ab=ab(a≥0,b>0)\sqrt{\frac{a}{b}} = \frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} \quad (a \ge 0, b > 0)ba​​=b​a​​(a≥0,b>0)

    The root of a quotient equals the quotient of the roots of the numerator and denominator.

    Legend:
    • a — Number a (a≥0)
    • b — Number b (b>0)

    Square of a root

    Grade 8
    (a)2=a(a≥0)(\sqrt{a})^2 = a \quad (a \ge 0)(a​)2=a(a≥0)

    Squaring the arithmetic square root.

    Legend:
    • a — Number a (a≥0)

    Root of a square

    Grade 8
    a2=∣a∣\sqrt{a^2} = |a|a2​=∣a∣

    The square root of the square of a number equals its absolute value.

    Legend:
    • a — Number a

    Negative exponent

    Grade 8
    a−n=1an(a≠0)a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n} \quad (a \neq 0)a−n=an1​(a=0)

    Replacing a negative exponent with the reciprocal value.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a (a≠0)
    • n — Exponent n

    Rational exponent

    Grade 10
    amn=amna^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m}anm​=nam​

    Expressing a power with a fractional exponent as a root.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a
    • m — Exponent m
    • n — Root index n (n≠0)

    Definition of a logarithm

    Grade 10
    log⁡ab=c  ⟺  ac=b(a>0,a≠1,b>0)\log_a b = c \iff a^c = b \quad (a>0, a \neq 1, b>0)loga​b=c⟺ac=b(a>0,a=1,b>0)

    Finds the exponent c to which base a must be raised to get b.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a (a>0, a≠1)
    • b — Argument b (b>0)

    Fundamental logarithmic identity

    Grade 10
    alog⁡ab=ba^{\log_a b} = baloga​b=b

    Raising base a to the power log_a(b) gives the argument b.

    Legend:
    • b — Number b (b>0)

    Properties of logarithms (sum)

    Grade 10
    log⁡ax+log⁡ay=log⁡a(x⋅y)\log_a x + \log_a y = \log_a (x \cdot y)loga​x+loga​y=loga​(x⋅y)

    The sum of logarithms equals the logarithm of the product of the arguments.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a
    • x — Argument x
    • y — Argument y

    Properties of logarithms (difference)

    Grade 10
    log⁡ax−log⁡ay=log⁡a(xy)\log_a x - \log_a y = \log_a \left(\frac{x}{y}\right)loga​x−loga​y=loga​(yx​)

    The difference of logarithms equals the logarithm of the quotient of the arguments.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a
    • x — Argument x
    • y — Argument y

    Taking an exponent out of a logarithm

    Grade 10
    log⁡a(xk)=k⋅log⁡ax\log_a (x^k) = k \cdot \log_a xloga​(xk)=k⋅loga​x

    The exponent k is moved out as a factor in front of the logarithm.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a
    • x — Argument x
    • k — Exponent k

    Change-of-base formula

    Grade 10
    log⁡ab=log⁡cblog⁡ca\log_a b = \frac{\log_c b}{\log_c a}loga​b=logc​alogc​b​

    Converting a logarithm with base a to base c.

    Legend:
    • a — Old base a
    • b — Argument b
    • c — New base c

    Logarithm of 1

    Grade 10
    log⁡a1=0\log_a 1 = 0loga​1=0

    The logarithm of 1 to any base is always 0.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a (a>0, a≠1)

    Logarithm of a number equal to the base

    Grade 10
    log⁡aa=1\log_a a = 1loga​a=1

    The logarithm of a number to an equal base is always 1.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a (a>0, a≠1)

    log⁡anx=1nlog⁡ax\log_{a^n} x = \frac{1}{n}\log_a xlogan​x=n1​loga​x

    Lets you move the exponent of the base out from under the logarithm (for n ≠ 0).

    Legend:
    • n — Exponent n
    • a — Base a (a>0, a≠1)
    • x — Argument x

    log⁡ax=1log⁡xa\log_a x = \frac{1}{\log_x a}loga​x=logx​a1​

    Relates the logarithm with base a to the logarithm with the reciprocal base.
