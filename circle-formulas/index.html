    Product of segments of two secants

    Grade 9
    AQ⋅BQ=CQ⋅DQAQ \cdot BQ = CQ \cdot DQAQ⋅BQ=CQ⋅DQ

    If two secants are drawn from a point outside a circle, the products of their segments are equal.

    Legend:
    • AQ — Segment AQ
    • BQ — Segment BQ
    • CQ — Segment CQ

    Grade 9

    The square of the tangent segment equals the product of the secant's segments, from the same point.

    • BQ — Segment BQ
