    Volume of a rectangular box

    Grade 5
    V=a⋅b⋅cV = a \cdot b \cdot cV=a⋅b⋅c

    Product of the three dimensions: length, width and height.

    Legend:
    • a — Length (a)
    • b — Width (b)
    • c — Height (c)

    Surface area of a rectangular box

    Grade 5
    S=2⋅(ab+bc+ac)S = 2 \cdot (ab + bc + ac)S=2⋅(ab+bc+ac)

    Sum of the areas of all six faces of the box.

    Legend:
    • a — Edge a
    • b — Edge b
    • c — Edge c

    Grade 5
    L=4⋅(a+b+c)L = 4 \cdot (a + b + c)L=4⋅(a+b+c)

    Sum of the lengths of all 12 edges of a rectangular box.

    Grade 5

    Volume of a prism

    Grade 10
    V=S⋅hV = S \cdot hV=S⋅h

    The volume of a prism equals the base area times the height.

    Legend:
    • S — Base area (S)
    • h — Height (h)
