    Area of a trapezoid

    Grade 8
    S=a+b2⋅hS = \frac{a + b}{2} \cdot hS=2a+b​⋅h

    The area of a trapezoid equals the product of the half-sum of the bases and the height.

    Legend:
    • a — Base a
    • b — Base b
    • h — Height h
