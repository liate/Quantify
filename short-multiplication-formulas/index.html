Short Multiplication Formulas

Short multiplication formulas let you expand and factor expressions without multiplying term by term: the square and cube of a sum or difference, the difference of squares, and the sum and difference of cubes. This page also lists the basic laws of arithmetic — commutative, associative and distributive — that every algebra rule is built on.

    Commutative law of addition

    Grade 5
    a+b=b+aa + b = b + aa+b=b+a

    Swapping the addends does not change the sum.

    Commutative law of multiplication

    Grade 5
    a⋅b=b⋅aa \cdot b = b \cdot aa⋅b=b⋅a

    Swapping the factors does not change the product.

    Associative law of addition

    Grade 5
    (a+b)+c=a+(b+c)(a+b)+c=a+(b+c)(a+b)+c=a+(b+c)

    The result of addition does not depend on how the addends are grouped.

    Legend:

    • c — Term c

    Associative law of multiplication

    Grade 5
    (a⋅b)⋅c=a⋅(b⋅c)(a \cdot b) \cdot c = a \cdot (b \cdot c)(a⋅b)⋅c=a⋅(b⋅c)

    The result of multiplication does not depend on how the factors are grouped.
